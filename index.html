⚖️ Core Principles

Engineering Philosophy

Modern computing has an abundance of RAM and GHz, but software keeps getting heavier. Here is how I build differently.

01

Native over Web Containers

Electron and heavyweight wrappers consume hundreds of megabytes of RAM just to render a button. Arcy Music and niri-x11 use pure Rust, direct X11/Wayland sockets, and Slint OpenGL hardware acceleration, cutting idle footprints to under 25 MB.

02

Microsecond Latency & Zero Stutter

Garbage collection pauses are unacceptable in window tiling and audiophile streaming. By moving windows directly without Cairo re-rasterization and caching PCM audio streams ahead of time, transitions remain pinned at 120 FPS.

03

Zero Telemetry & Absolute Privacy

Zero tracking, zero cloud dependencies, zero external analytics. Local SQLite databases store user playlists and settings strictly inside standard XDG directories. Credential tokens are isolated and masked.

04

Tactile Visual & Audio Aesthetics

Performance doesn't mean boring ASCII. From Catppuccin Mocha and Tokyo Night color schemes to real-time 5-band biquad equalizers and glassmorphic HUDs, every interface is engineered to feel alive and satisfying to touch.
